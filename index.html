Determining protein in food: the Kjeldahl method

Nitrogen is measured in three steps, then converted to protein. Tap a step to jump to it.

1 Digestion2 Distillation3 Titration4 Calculation

1 Digestion

1 g sample + 1 g K₂SO₄ catalyst + 20 mL conc. H₂SO₄, heated until colorless, then diluted to 250 mL. Repeat without sample for the blank.

Heating

Protein + H₂SO₄ → CO₂ + H₂O + (NH₄)₂SO₄
Result: all nitrogen is now ammonium sulfate. Colors: tan → black → brown → colorless.

2 Distillation

10 mL digest + 10 mL 45% NaOH, about 15 min. Ammonia is collected in 10 mL 2% boric acid with 4 drops of Tashiro indicator.

Time

(NH₄)₂SO₄ + 2 NaOH → 2 NH₃ + 2 H₂O + Na₂SO₄
NH₃ + H₃BO₃ → NH₄⁺ + H₂BO₃⁻
Result: receiver goes purple-pink → grey → green as ammonia is captured as borate.

3 Titration

Fill the burette with 0.02 N HCl and titrate the receiving flask until purple appears. Repeat for the blank.

HCl added

H₂BO₃⁻ + H⁺ → H₃BO₃
Result: green → grey → purple at the end point. Record the HCl volume for sample and blank.

4 Calculation

Change the values to see each step, in grams. 1 mL of 0.02 N HCl = 0.00028 g N.
